Meet the team
Three people, one palette.
Creative Director
Christian Alvez
Specializing in videography, photography and branding. He combines visual storytelling with thoughtful design to create work that captures attention and communicates with purpose. From cinematic films and striking imagery to digital identities and engaging websites, his work is built to make brands seen, felt and remembered.
Director of Photography
Briggs Tameta
With a focus on cinematography, photography, video editing, color grading and branding. He creates visual work that goes beyond simply looking good. Every frame, color, cut, and design element is carefully crafted to communicate a story and give each brand its own distinct characters.
Project Manager
Derek Limbo
Specializing in project management, SEO, digital marketing strategy and trend research. He oversees the planning and execution of creative projects from concept to completion. He keeps the team aligned, manages timelines and deliverables and stays ahead of emerging trends, viral content and audience behavior to develop marketing strategies that keep brands relevant and competitive.
